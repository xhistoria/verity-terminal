# Thesis

## User

Robinhood Chain users who discover assets or wallet activity and want a safer way to execute trades without handing private keys to a bot.

## Painful job

Move from an on-chain signal to a real trade while understanding route, spender, minimum output, price impact, fees, simulation result, and settlement state.

## Product promise

**Understand first. Execute with limits. Verify settlement.**

## Wedge

A Robinhood Chain social execution inbox: actionable on-chain events plus evidence, a simulated follower outcome, one-tap user-confirmed execution, and an auditable receipt.

## Non-goals

No server custody, seed/private-key handling, arbitrary contract calls, guaranteed-profit claims, unrestricted copytrading, volume manipulation, or RWA/Stock Token execution before legal and restriction review.

## Success hypothesis

Users will repeatedly return for explainable event alerts and complete small, user-confirmed trades when total cost and risk are transparent.
