ROUTE POLICY

One verified path

Only ETH → USDG through the pinned Uniswap V3 pool and SwapRouter02 is enabled. Arbitrary tokens and contracts are rejected.

SIGNING BOUNDARY

Your wallet, your approval

The server prepares and simulates calldata. Only your connected wallet can sign and broadcast the transaction.

SETTLEMENT TRUTH

Hash is not success

Broadcast, pending, receipt-confirmed, reverted, and unknown remain separate states. This beta requires a successful receipt and does not claim Ethereum finality.

PUBLIC BETA SCOPE

This release intentionally excludes unattended copytrading, server-held keys, token launches, and Stock Token/RWA execution. Start small and verify every wallet prompt.
