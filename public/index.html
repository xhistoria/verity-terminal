BOUNDARIES, NOT PROMISES

Evidence before action.
Truth after broadcast.

01

ADAPTER POLICY

Many markets, closed compilers

Hookless and explicitly verified hook classes can earn separate adapters. Unknown hooks stay visible but blocked; arbitrary commands, hookData, recipients, and contracts are never passed through.

02

SIGNING BOUNDARY

Your wallet, your approval

The server prepares and simulates calldata. Only your connected wallet can sign and broadcast the transaction.

03

SETTLEMENT TRUTH

Hash is not success

Broadcast, pending, receipt-confirmed, reverted, and unknown remain separate states. A receipt is required; full finality is not claimed.

LOCAL EXECUTION EVIDENCE

Receipts, not claims.

Verity stores a bounded journal in this browser after broadcast. It records expected execution evidence and updates only from Robinhood Chain receipts.

No locally recorded executions
No execution receipts yet A simulation is not an execution. Entries appear only after your wallet returns a transaction hash.
CONSTRAINED PREVIEW SCOPE
